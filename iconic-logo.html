Fifteen versions in twenty minutes

Iconic launched with a placeholder that said nothing about the app, so my brief was one line: a square and a circle, dotted like they're mid-edit. I designed it in conversation with Claude, one short direction per live version. The first draft's handles and round dots came out, long right-angle corners went in, and optical fixes made the two shapes look equal.

Small sizes forced the biggest change. At 16px the dashes blurred to grey, so the square went solid under a white circle and became v2. Shadows, 45° angles and the full four-color header gradient all felt busy or dated. v3 keeps one moment of my animated header, coral fading to peach, and v2 stays as the lo-fi mark in Iconic's toolbar.
