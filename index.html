About

I'm Ray Santos, a designer in the New York City area who cares about the smallest pixels in a system.

Currently I design for Panasonic North America and run Raysan Designs. Before that I drew OSG's fintech icon sets and led its 2024 brand refresh, and spent nine years at PNY Technologies growing into senior web designer. I trained in communication design at Pratt.

Working on small teams taught me to own the whole path: the research, the system, the pixels and the handoff to engineering. I work with US and global teams, remote and in person, and I love illustration and drawing custom icons.

Away from the screen I'm cooking, drawing with my kids, designing furniture and woodworking with hand tools and machinery.
