Resin Matrix

Anycubic sells 17 resins, and its own comparison page made it hard to tell them apart. So I built a sortable table with every resin, every published spec and the current price.

The official comparison is built for browsing, not choosing

Anycubic's page puts resins across the top, so the table runs about 4,160 pixels wide: nearly three laptop screens of sideways scrolling. It can't be sorted, has no prices, still listed two discontinued resins and missed two new ones. Comparing even two resins meant scrolling back and forth and holding numbers in my head.

Seventeen spec sheets, one table

I pulled 14 properties plus the 1 kg price from each product's Specification tab, and side by side the numbers exposed problems.

  • check_circleAnycubic swaps “bending modulus” and “bending strength” on every page; the matrix uses the correct names.
  • check_circleWhere a store listing disagreed with the spec sheet, I used the spec sheet and noted it.
  • check_circleValues stay as published ranges, like 35–45 MPa, not flattened to one number.

Every option first, then the differences

All 17 resins are on screen from the start, with no picker, because seeing the whole range is how you learn it.

  • check_circleOne resin per row makes all sixteen properties sortable columns that fit a large desktop screen.
  • check_circleComparing two resins takes a glance across rows, not three screens of scrolling.
  • check_circleA star marks each column's standout, and seven leader cards answer the common questions.
  • check_circleFamily, water-wash and sold-out filters narrow the list only when you ask.

Rows that move to their new place

When the table re-sorts, each row slides to its new spot, so you can follow a resin and see how far it moved. Clicking a leader card sorts by that property and scrolls to the table. All motion is skipped when a device asks for reduced motion.

Answers in place, not in a glossary

MPa and J/m only help if you know what they mean for a print, so explanations appear where the questions come up.

  • check_circleChoosing a family fades in a plain note on its strengths and tradeoffs.
  • check_circleHovering a column explains the property, its unit and whether higher or lower is better.
  • check_circleHovering a resin's name lists what it's recommended for.

Styled like Anycubic's store, usable on a phone

I borrowed the store's live styles so the matrix feels like it belongs there: the #005AFF blue, pill buttons, light gray panels and MiSans Latin with Inter as fallback. Light and dark modes share the blue and switch with one button. On a phone the table scrolls sideways, snapping to columns, while resin names stay pinned on the left.

Most of the edits were deletions

The first version said everything at once, so I reviewed it as a buyer would and took things away.

  • check_circleRange bars under each value went first: they changed no decision and doubled cell height.
  • check_circleUnits and “higher is better” notes moved into header tooltips, so headers fit on one line.
  • check_circleUse notes became a name tooltip; wash method and odor got their own sortable columns.
  • check_circleThe intro, eyebrow and section heading came out; title, date and table say enough.

Researched, designed and shipped in a day

I built the Resin Matrix with Claude in one day. Claude gathered the specs, read the store's styles, wrote the page and published it to GitHub; I set the direction and reviewed every change on the live page. With changes live in minutes, I could judge each one on the real page and cut what wasn't helping.

Things to improve

  • radio_button_uncheckedOther brands. Adding Elegoo, Siraya Tech, Phrozen and others, with brand as a filter, would make it a true buyer's guide.
  • radio_button_uncheckedA shortlist, after the full picture. A step that pins two or three favorites and compares only those would finish the decision.
  • radio_button_uncheckedKeep the data current. Prices and specs are a one-day snapshot, and a scheduled script that re-reads the product pages would keep them fresh.
  • radio_button_uncheckedA better phone layout. Sideways scrolling shows only a few columns, and cards with each resin's key numbers would suit a phone better.
