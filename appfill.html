AppFill

Job applications ask for the same details in a slightly different form each time. I designed and built a Chrome extension that fills them from one right-click and outlines whatever it can't answer.

Same answers, every application

Most of my time on each application went to retyping answers I had already given. Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday all want name, email, phone and LinkedIn, then ask what Chrome's autofill never touches: work authorization, sponsorship, years of experience. Picking portfolio pieces and writing to the hiring team got whatever time was left.

Fill from the right-click, outline the gaps

Autofill lives in the menu already under my hand, with no popup or per-site setup.

  • check_circleFill blanks, never overwrite. Only empty fields change, so running it twice is harmless.
  • check_circleShow the gaps. Anything it can't place gets a dashed amber outline, not a guess.
  • check_circleLearn each platform once. Answers saved on one Greenhouse posting fill the next one.
  • check_circleStays on this computer. No account or server; hidden trap fields and ID or bank answers are never filled or saved.

Most of a form in one click

I set the behavior, Claude wrote the code, then it ran in Chromium against a Greenhouse-style test form.

  • check_circle13 of 14 fields filled on the first pass, with no saved answers.
  • check_circle15 of 15 filled after remembering the site once.
  • check_circle0 password, file or unsaved checkbox fields touched.
